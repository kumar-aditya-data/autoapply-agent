Description
FreeTextbooks uses student influencers to overthrow inefficient and overpriced bookstores, cutting textbook costs in half. Founded in 2009, we’ve profitably posted 50% growth every year, yet our 300 reps across 30 campuses barely scratch the potential in 20 million students.We’re passionate about what we’re building, and this position takes our growth to the next level. Starting immediately, we need a new team member to lead our warehousing, inventory control, purchasing, campus delivery, and shipping carriers. Seasonally, this involves:orchestrating the flow of textbooks from our 30 campuses to one central distribution center; leading the purchasing, scanning, processing &amp; shipping thousands of textbooks;building relational communication with campus rep teams; training, leading &amp; developing seasonal part-time teams; systematizing elements of sourcing, distribution &amp; quality control of textbooks;Flexibility &amp; a desire to learn is of utmost importance, as this position will wear many hats. As a result of rapid growth, there's a huge need to establish more finite processes within our supply chain &amp; fulfillment before we scale even more quickly. We expect to triple our growth in 2015, and this hire is our next step toward that.
